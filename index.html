How to Play RedZombies

1. Move: Use Arrow Keys, WASD, tap grid cells, swipe, or use the glass D-pad to navigate across the 3×3 grid.

2. Warning Sweeps: When a row or column pulses red, a lethal strike beam is charging. The arrow outside shows where it attacks from.

3. Take Cover: Stand behind grey shield barricades or step out of the danger line before the beam fires.

4. Oracle Protocol: When reduced to 2 lifelines, predict the next strike direction (North/South/East/West) to restore a shield.

5. Streaks: Every 5 waves survived without taking a hit earns +3 bonus points and an emerald aura.

6. Global Leaderboard: Your high score syncs worldwide in real time via Supabase PostgreSQL!
